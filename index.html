What We Offer at Latino Lighthouse

Choose the path that fits your life and goals.
Every offering is designed to take you from understanding Spanish to speaking it with ease and joy.

Beginner Classes

Start comfortably with a teacher who speaks your native language. Full immersion from day one.

Free Level Test

Not sure of your level? Take our free 8-minute test and get personalized recommendations instantly.

Literary Workshops

Explore literature while practicing conversation. Improve comprehension, vocabulary and cultural understanding.

Business Spanish

Live classes with a certified teacher & lawyer, or Self-Study pre-recorded course for busy professionals.

Casual Conversation Practice

Relaxed guided sessions to build confidence and natural fluency in a supportive environment.

Masterclasses on Specific Topics

Focused sessions on the subjunctive, verb tenses, connectors, and other challenging grammar areas.

Spanish for Tourism & Migration

Practical language training for traveling or moving to a Spanish-speaking country. Real-life situations.

Cultural Events

Presentations about life, customs and expressions from different Latin American countries. Fun & enriching.

Cinema Together

We meet online to watch Spanish films together, laugh, debate and learn real colloquial expressions.
